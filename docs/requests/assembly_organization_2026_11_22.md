# November 22 assembly organization planning selection

The owner requests: "Ok let’s next update the tickets and experimental plan for
this. We probably shouldn’t call it hysteresis since that’s not exactly the right
name for it".

The selected name is **Assembly organization and selective routing**, planning
identifier AO-1. [SOAM-103](../../tickets/SOAM-103-assembly-organization-plan.md)
records this fresh routing choice and the
[experimental plan](../proposed-studies/STUDY_ASSEMBLY_ORGANIZATION.md).

The discussion specifies that hierarchy concerns learned connectivity and effective
influence, rather than temporal hysteresis alone. A modest feature vector may
drive a larger population through a dense fixed projection; hard-masked entry
neurons, prescribed roots/leaves, and supplied assembly boundaries are not required
and are not the primary scaffold. Competition, activity regulation, recurrent
reinforcement, and directional transfer are candidate organizing ingredients.
Their exact learning and stability rules must be derived independently of the
2014 assembly paper. No claim of inevitable hierarchy or semantic organization
is adopted.

The owner wants learned organization included in the first mechanism question.
The sequence is equations and analytical checks, small bounded simulations, and a
frozen experiment. Lean is optional for a valuable theorem once the assumptions
and mechanism are stable. The prior preference to keep hysteresis thresholds fixed
still applies if that optional mechanism is included; it does not prescribe neural
roles or imply that hysteresis is required.

This request authorizes updating the plan and tickets. SOAM-104 is the next design
task, followed conditionally by SOAM-105–107. No implementation, fitted exploratory
search, confirmation, remote issue publication, or blog publication is performed
by this planning task. Existing authorization governs later work without an added
human approval layer; a complete numerical contract is still required before fits.

The earlier SOAM-102 decision remains done/proceed for its completed E0-HYS design.
Its protocol and numerical manifest remain preserved, unrun, and currently
unselected. It is not a prerequisite for testing organization. This new selection
supersedes its execution priority and its blanket deferral of assembly-routing
research, not its mathematical retention question or any measured result.

This is a narrow scope amendment for an offline mechanism pilot. SOAM-042/043's
integrated organization and acquisition/sharing prerequisites remain unchanged.
E0's inconclusive outcome, E0-HOLD's negative joint fix, and E1's sharing stop are
preserved. Classification: AO-1 mathematical/protocol design is **essential next**;
Lean is **optional supporting research**; online growth, full integration, and
biological or general semantic claims remain **future capabilities**.
