# Codex prompt — Optional C2: Primed/tracking/attended selective processing

**Status:** deferred concept. Study C-H1 did not authorize C-H2 scheduling or C-H3 learned attention. Treat this document as a *conditional* bounded experiment only when a new routing decision establishes a compute or continuity limitation.

## Research question

Can independent control over recurrent/sensorimotor updates make two or more **simultaneously valid** component states cheaper to maintain without degrading predictions when processing priority shifts?

Do not reuse Study C's two *competing* regime hypotheses as if both physical regimes coexist. Build a separate environment with two independently moving objects or component processes. Do not normalize complementary existence probabilities into a mutually exclusive two-class distribution.

## Evidence and controls

TWO16 beat PC16 and PC32 on Study C's candidate Brier metric, but both recurrent states were fully updated and the p95 latency premium versus PC32 was only ~12%. Conventional ADAPT1-C remained competitive; the coherent-cause failure was about omitted hypotheses/information, not wasted computation. Thus selective scheduling is not a proven need.

Freeze representation first. Compare at matched evidence, total state and measured compute:
- full updates of both components;
- freeze unattended component;
- motion-only tracking without costly sensory correction;
- round-robin updates with explicit elapsed-time catch-up;
- fixed uncertainty/innovation-triggered updates;
- learned scheduling **only** if simple policies show a measurable bottleneck.

Define independently controllable sensory priority, action propagation, recurrent inference iterations and attention/readout priority. “Primed,” “tracking,” “attended” are *interpretations* of experimentally useful gate configurations, not required discrete modes. Never erase a component on attention switch or reset from simulator truth. Give the controller no oracle object ID or future cue; externally supplied focus schedules are labeled scaffold conditions.

Use independently changing components, occlusion, distraction and re-attention. Measure retained object identity, stale-state error, drift while unattended, immediate and delayed prediction after switching, missed observations, interference, catch-up work, avg/p95 latency, retained state and total compute. Protect accuracy before claiming resource savings; test each candidate at matched resource and along a Pareto frontier. Include observation-availability vs compute-allocation factorial controls. A learned controller must beat simple heuristics under frozen confirmation data.

No persistent learning, thalamic messaging or intermingled coupled settling in C2; those need their own protocols.

Before fitting, freeze protocol, roots, temporal schedules, endpoints, meaningful effects, guards and resource limits. Preserve negatives and historical C decision. Deliver report/decision only; do not automatically activate D/E/F.

**Final question:** Is there an empirical reason for explicit primed, tracking and attended computation, rather than ordinary full updating or simpler deterministic scheduling?
