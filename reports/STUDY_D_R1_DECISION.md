# Study D-R1 decision

November 18, 2026. Owned by [SOAM-089](../tickets/SOAM-089-recurrent-retrieval-decision.md).

**Proceed with acceptance of the bounded evidence and retain KV as the conventional
reference for this scaffolded task. PH768 does not pass reliable retrieval or the
practical additional-settling claim.** This decision completes the selected study;
it does not authorize a successor experiment or claim a positive mechanism gate.

to ten fresh independent confirmation roots. All 16 allowed core fits and all
mandatory arms complete. Development competence and resource feasibility passed;
settings and independent calibration preceded confirmation. No failed attempts,
exclusions, repair, extra roots or confirmation tuning were introduced. Repeat and
independent reduction pass. The cumulative ledger remains within the explicitly
accepted allocation. This is valid execution, so scientific classification applies.

## What failed, and what remains open

Do not equate recall interference with proven destruction of stored codes. Full
appearance cues raise PH768 accuracy only from .70312 to .71607, so missing key
coordinates alone do not account for the problem. The post hoc audit of all ten
available final checkpoints (fourth sequence only) finds all 64 acquired codes
preserved in the projector span to ≤1.78e-15 residual. This limited algebraic audit
supports storage preservation in those checkpoints; it does not establish correct
completion from the query, unique association identity, useful basins or a decoder
that preserves the relevant relationships.

The candidate converged rapidly but often to an incorrect output. The small
activity-flip diagnostic does not establish useful trajectory recovery. Supplied
encoding, correspondence, roles, clamps and action basis remain scaffolding.
No learned organization, shared-neuron advantage, general capacity/scaling law,
biological validation or general rejection of recurrent memory follows.

## One next recommendation, not an activated fork

Recommend **one fresh, fixed-size retrieval-versus-decoding diagnostic protocol**.
Its narrow question is whether an already preserved association is recoverable
through PH768's present completion and readout path. Use fresh streams and pair
the unchanged cue-only retrieval with a declared evaluator-only exact acquired-code
decode check, separated from learner inference. Record both code completion error
and decoded relationship error at the same fixed load. This separates a faulty or
insufficient representation/readout from failure to reach a useful stored code;
the privileged decode is a diagnostic ceiling and cannot count as deployed recall.
Retain KV and REG-LIFT references and predefine criteria and a finite allocation
before any fitting. Do not run an architecture, clamp, capacity or encoding sweep.

This recommendation has higher immediate information value than building coupled
populations or learned organization: it addresses the observed recall failure
while preserving the current mechanism. It is the only follow-up recommended here,
not a selected ticket or an approved protocol. Fresh routing is required before
implementation. D3/D4, E0/F and all other branches remain deferred; historical
SOAM-052 stop, SOAM-069 revise and earlier study decisions are unchanged.
