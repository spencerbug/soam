# E0 decision: accept the bounded evidence; address stationary drift before coupling

2026-11-21 · SOAM-097 · **decision: proceed with evidence acceptance only**.
The [E0 report](STUDY_E0.md) completes the [owner-selected](../docs/requests/e0_2026_11_21.md)
single-population study. The joint navigable-geometry criterion did not pass.
This decision does not authorize the next experiment or change historical gates.

NAV32 passes blindfolded action prediction, grounded prediction, local transverse recovery,
perturbation-induced pose preservation, average tangential separation retention and
returning-cue correction. Its long stationary hold remains the limiting endpoint:
13.861° mean pose error after 128 steps, adjusted interval [12.119°,16.278°]. The
entire interval is above the 5° target. Its 4.159° width nevertheless exceeds the
preregistered 4° cap, preserving the formal **inconclusive** outcome. We do not
relax that cap or recast the unfavorable direction as a passing result.

The evidence is valid within its scope: 58 scientific fits, all ten confirmation
roots, frozen development selection, 108 exact repeated arrays, 29 exact repeated
tensors, 280 independently reduced root metrics, 28 checked adjusted intervals,
matching source/data/payload hashes and no failed/excluded runs. Cumulative numerical
and audit costs remain below every ceiling. The final raw-state/metric audit does
not refit models or reuse confirmation as tuning data. The reviewed
[receipt](study-e0/receipt.json) and [archive inventory](study-e0/archive_sha256.json)
preserve provenance; the full 155 MB archive remains local, not published.

Retain NAV32 as a **candidate with an identified persistence limitation**, not a
validated continuous attractor. The conventional PCA2 filter is the strongest
prediction/state-size reference for this supplied rank-two task. Its transverse
ratio of one shows that accurate tracking does not require autonomous attraction.
GRU32 is a mandatory neural comparator: it also contracts transverse perturbations,
although this fixed-budget configuration precisely misses the blindfolded action criterion (64 steps).
SYM32's high action error is imprecise under the frozen cap. Those findings neither
establish Hopfield superiority nor rule out better trained conventional recurrence.

**Essential next, conditionally recommended under SOAM-097:** one fresh corrective
E0 test of longer zero-action training against the existing short-horizon training
condition. Freeze a new protocol, fresh roots, resources, primary 128-step pose
retention and action/recovery guards before execution. This changes one training
factor and can test whether the 24-step training horizon contributes to accumulated
drift. Current trajectories motivate that hypothesis but do not prove the cause.
The recommendation does not specify optimized settings from confirmation or spend
the remaining v1 budget. No successor ticket or experiment is activated here.

Cross-module translation/correction, flat RIM-like routing and hierarchical systems
remain **future capabilities**. E0 has not yet supplied a core that passes both
local recovery and long stationary pose retention. Capacity, HTM/sparse encoders,
shared reference frames, multiple objects, persistent acquisition and learned module
organization are outside this experiment. No additional parallel numerical branch
is selected. SOAM-052 stop, SOAM-069 revise, D-R1 negative evidence and D-R2's
inconclusive diagnosis remain intact; KV's prior storage-reference role is unchanged.

This is a substantive candidate for an accompanying blog/report: the measured
distinction between **local recovery, accurate prediction and stationary memory**
is useful even without a passed joint gate. Any article should prominently retain
the formal inconclusive outcome and the roughly 14° drift. The owner is notified;
no publication, remote push or issue synchronization is authorized by this review.
